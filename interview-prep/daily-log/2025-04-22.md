### Q1. Explain quicksort and its worst case.

**Answer.** Pick a pivot, partition around it, recurse. Average O(n log n), in-place, cache-friendly. Worst case O(n^2) on sorted input with naive first/last pivot — fixed by randomized or median-of-three pivot. It's not stable.
