### Q1. What is the difference between BFS and Dijkstra on unweighted graphs?

**Answer.** On unweighted graphs Dijkstra degenerates to BFS — every relaxation has the same unit cost, so the heap never reorders anything. BFS is the simpler, faster choice there; keep Dijkstra for weighted edges.
