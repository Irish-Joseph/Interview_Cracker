### Q1. What is normalization and when would you denormalize?

**Answer.** Normalization (1NF..3NF, BCNF) removes redundancy and update anomalies by decomposing relations and keying facts. Denormalize when read latency matters more than write consistency — e.g. embedding an author's name in a posts table or a materialized aggregation — accepting that some write path must keep them in sync.
