### Q1. Explain the divide and conquer pattern.

**Answer.** Split the problem into independent subproblems, solve each recursively, combine the results. The recurrence is T(n) = aT(n/b) + O(n^d); the Master Theorem compares a/b^d to decide the bound. Examples: merge sort, matrix multiplication, closest pair.

---

### Q2. How do you find the kth largest element faster than sorting?

**Answer.** Quickselect: partition like quicksort but recurse only into the side containing the kth element — average O(n), worst O(n^2) (fix with median-of-medians pivot or randomization). Or a size-k min-heap: O(n log k), which wins when k << n.
