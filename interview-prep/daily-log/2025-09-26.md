### Q1. How do you find the kth largest element faster than sorting?

**Answer.** Quickselect: partition like quicksort but recurse only into the side containing the kth element — average O(n), worst O(n^2) (fix with median-of-medians pivot or randomization). Or a size-k min-heap: O(n log k), which wins when k << n.
