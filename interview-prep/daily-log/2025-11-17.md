### Q1. What is virtual memory?

**Answer.** Each process sees a private address space backed by the MMU, which maps virtual pages to physical frames via page tables. It gives isolation, overcommit (map more than RAM), lazy allocation, and the foundation for demand paging and copy-on-write fork.
