### Q1. Memoization vs tabulation?

**Answer.** Memoization is top-down recursion with a cache; tabulation is bottom-up iteration filling a table. Same complexity; tabulation removes recursion overhead and can enable space reduction (keep only the last 2 rows), while memoization is often easier to derive from the recurrence.

---

### Q2. What is the difference between a stack and a queue?

**Answer.** A stack is LIFO — push/pop both happen at the same end; a queue is FIFO — enqueue at the back, dequeue from the front. Stacks model undo, call frames, backtracking; queues model task scheduling, BFS, rate limiting.
