### Q1. What is a covering index?

**Answer.** An index that contains all columns the query needs (e.g. INCLUDE in PostgreSQL, or a composite wide enough), so the query is answered from the index alone — no heap 'bookmark lookups'. Common in OLTP for 'index-only scans' on hot read paths.

---

### Q2. What is sharding and how do you choose a shard key?

**Answer.** Partition data across databases by a key: hash sharding spreads evenly (but range queries hit all shards), range sharding keeps locality (but risks hot ranges). Choose the key from your query patterns — a hot key (e.g. one user's 1B feed) skews load, so you may double-key (user_id, post_id).
