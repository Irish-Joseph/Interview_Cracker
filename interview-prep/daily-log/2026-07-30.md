# Daily Interview Prep — July 30, 2026

Focus: databases. 1 question worked through this session.

### Q1. How do you find the kth largest element faster than sorting?

**Answer.** Quickselect: partition like quicksort but recurse only into the side containing the kth element — average O(n), worst O(n^2) (fix with median-of-medians pivot or randomization). Or a size-k min-heap: O(n log k), which wins when k << n.

---

### Q2. What is a skip list and why do databases use it?

**Answer.** A sorted linked list with extra 'express lanes' at higher levels, giving O(log n) expected search with much simpler, lock-friendly code than a balanced tree. LevelDB/RocksDB use it for their memtable because range scans are easy and concurrent insertion is cheap.
